Efficiency / Our approach

Quality sets the bar.
We optimize the cost.

The quality professional work requires is non-negotiable. We use highly optimized local models built on open-source foundations and assign different models and agents to each stage to optimize token use and inference costs.

Token efficiency, measured per outcome
Our goal is to lower the total cost of producing the result a customer needs at the required quality, making specialized AI economical for repeated use.
Economics from delivery to operation
Reusing core technology reduces development and delivery effort. Matching models to workflow stages targets operating costs. Together, these strategies aim to make fragmented professional markets viable to serve.
