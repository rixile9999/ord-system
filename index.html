04 / The platform in practice

RN-AI.
Agentic AI for
nurse scheduling.

From existing spreadsheets to a reviewable roster. RN-AI connects conversational requests and workspace editing to structured ward data and constraint-based scheduling.

Context-aware tools
UI and agent operations share domain services for staff, shift codes, and requests. Current screen context informs the conversation.
Constraint programming
Staffing, work patterns, skills, and preferences are represented through hard constraints and weighted penalties in OR-Tools CP-SAT.
Solver-independent evaluation
Candidate and edited rosters can be evaluated for rule violations and penalty breakdowns without running a new optimization.
